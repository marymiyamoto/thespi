# Privacy Policy **Effective date:** June 1, 2026 **Last updated:** June 1, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Thespi ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Thespi mobile application (the "App"). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. --- ## 1. Who We Are Thespi is a social platform for rating, reviewing, and discovering live theater. The App is operated under the name **Thespi**. For privacy-related questions, contact us at **privacy@thespiapp.com**. --- ## 2. Information We Collect ### Information you provide directly - **Account information:** When you register, we collect your display name, email address, and a password (stored as a one-way hash — we cannot read it). - **Profile information:** Optionally, a profile photo, a short bio, and your favorite genres. - **Reviews and ratings:** The shows you mark as seen, your sentiment ratings (loved / fine / didn't enjoy), written reviews, and any photos you attach to a review. - **Social activity:** Likes you give to other users' reviews and comments you post. ### Information generated by your use of the App - **Show interactions:** Shows you mark as "Want to See," your comparison rankings, and your personal rating history. - **Session identifiers:** A random authentication token stored on your device to keep you logged in. We do not store your password in plain text at any point. ### Information we do NOT collect - Location data - Contacts or address book - Device identifiers beyond what is required for push notification delivery (if you grant permission) - Payment information (the App is currently free) --- ## 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: - Create and maintain your account - Display your ratings, reviews, and profile to other users of the App - Generate personalized show recommendations based on your genre preferences and rating history - Allow other users to like and comment on your public reviews - Operate and improve the App We do not sell your personal information. --- ## 4. Information Shared With Others ### Public by default The following content is visible to all users of the App: - Your display name and profile photo - Reviews and ratings you submit - Photos you attach to reviews - Likes and comments you make ### Service providers We may share information with third-party service providers who help us operate the App (for example, cloud hosting). These providers are contractually required to protect your information and may only use it to provide services on our behalf. ### Advertising We do not currently serve ads. We reserve the right to introduce advertising in the future, in which case this policy will be updated and you will be notified in advance. ### Legal requirements We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal process. --- ## 5. Data Retention We retain your account and content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it longer. --- ## 6. Your Rights and Choices You have the right to: - **Access** the personal information we hold about you - **Correct** inaccurate information via your profile settings - **Delete** your account and associated data by contacting us at privacy@thespiapp.com - **Withdraw consent** for push notifications at any time through your device settings To exercise any of these rights, email us at **privacy@thespiapp.com**. --- ## 7. Children's Privacy Thespi is intended for users aged **13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@thespiapp.com and we will delete it promptly. --- ## 8. Security We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including password hashing and encrypted data transmission (HTTPS). No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. --- ## 9. Third-Party Services The App uses **Wikipedia's public API** to retrieve show information, track listings, and images. Wikipedia's own terms of use and privacy policy apply to those requests. We do not share your personal information with Wikipedia. --- ## 10. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, notify you through the App or by email. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. --- ## 11. Contact Us If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us: **Email:** privacy@thespiapp.com --- *This policy covers the Thespi mobile application only.*
